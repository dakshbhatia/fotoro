# Fotoro roadmap

The [active product queue](../../fotoro/docs/product-backlog.md) is the single
roadmap. The complete current build lives in `fotoro/`; see
[run instructions](../../fotoro/README.md), [verification](../../fotoro/docs/verification.md)
and [release setup](../../fotoro/docs/deployment.md).

1. **Finish the existing journey.** Reach older filtered Photos, reconcile ended
   sharing access, and retain real browser HEIC capture dates where metadata exists.
   Preserve selections, original bytes and independently saved copies.
2. **Deliver the full core.** Clear the accurate Apple declaration, verify
   TestFlight access, and qualify a complete phone → Safari and two-person journey.
3. **Improve useful intelligence.** Evaluate local visual retrieval and Picks
   against fixed, held-out examples. Add correctable People after naming and
   permission-change behavior are defined. Keep indexing away from scrolling.
4. **Complete media backup.** Live Photo resource pairs, video playback and
   interrupted original restore belong to one deliverable and the current queue.
5. **Add safe cleanup.** Recoverable trash and undo precede deleting photos or
   freeing phone storage; restored original bytes must be verified first.

Reuse the current SwiftUI, React, Cloudflare, account, catalog and durable transfer
queue. Add no second index, queue, framework or inference provider merely to expand
the feature list. Optional cloud AI needs a specific measured capability, opt-in
and a spending limit. Whole-library responsiveness, personal device behavior and
release availability require separate evidence; synthetic tests do not prove them.

Earlier five-slice Ente proposals and dated model/price recommendations are
historical plans in Git. They no longer form another active implementation queue.
